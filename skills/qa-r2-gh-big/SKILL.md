# Ticket summary

1. Read the ticket subject and description.
2. Write exactly three bullets: the problem, the impact, the next step.
3. Keep each bullet under 25 words.
4. Do not invent facts that are not in the ticket.

See references/f1.md to references/f6.md for background.
